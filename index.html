Core Capabilities & Services
  • Water Supply Schemes (JJM & AMRUT 2.0): Execution of rural and urban water supply networks, including intake wells, sumps, overhead storage tanks (OHTs up to 800 KL capacity), and extensive HDPE/CI/DI pipeline laying.
  • Sewage & Wastewater Treatment (SBM 2.0): Turnkey design, civil construction, pumping stations, drainage networks, and long-term O&M for STPs (0.5 MLD to 3.0 MLD) under SBM and UADD initiatives.
  • Civil & Commercial Infrastructure: Development of residential layouts, rigid pavement roads, commercial buildings, and government office facilities.
Key Equipment & Plant Infrastructure
  • Earthmoving Equipment: Backhoe Loaders, Tractors, and heavy-duty Road Breakers for trenching and rock breaking.
  • Concrete Infrastructure: Self-loading Ready-Mix Concrete (RMC) for structural pours.
  • Formwork & Shuttering: 200+ sets of specialized shuttering suited for both round and square sump construction and storage reservoirs.
